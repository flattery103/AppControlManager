AppControl Manager 1.0.0-rc.13

- Configures Windows Service Control Manager crash recovery for both the main agent
  and the Local Service Rule Worker.
- Restarts an unexpectedly terminated service after 10 seconds, then 30 seconds,
  then 60 seconds, and resets the failure counter after 24 hours.
- Applies the recovery contract during new installation, repair, manual
  upgrade, managed agent update, and normal agent startup so existing RC12
  endpoints are repaired automatically when RC13 starts.
- Adds RC13-versioned health and disruptive recovery validators. Health mode
  verifies the configured SCM actions; CrashRecovery mode proves the services
  actually restart after forced termination.

Security behavior is unchanged. A deliberate administrative service stop does
not trigger automatic restart, and no Windows App Control policy is weakened.
