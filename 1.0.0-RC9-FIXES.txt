AppControl Manager 1.0.0-rc.9

- Adds deletion for paused or completed automatic update deployment policies.
- Prevents active policies and policies with pending endpoint work from being deleted.
- Preserves agent update history when an obsolete deployment policy is removed.
- Automatically retires a successful one-time manual device update assignment.
- Immediately recalculates affected devices after retirement or deletion so broader Latest Stable policies can take over.
- Prevents a completed manual update from permanently pinning a device to an old release.
